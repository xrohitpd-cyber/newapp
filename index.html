What I run

Five areas of responsibility, each with a clear owner, a clear target and a plan for when something breaks.

  • Data center and compute

    Capacity planning, virtualization, hardware refresh cycles and vendor contracts for on-premises and colocation environments.

  • Network and connectivity

    LAN, WAN and SD-WAN design, firewall policy, wireless coverage and ISP management across every site.

  • Cloud and hybrid platforms

    Landing zones, workload migration, cost control and a steady path from legacy servers to managed services.

  • Security and compliance

    Patching, endpoint protection, access reviews and audit readiness, built into daily operations instead of added at the end.

  • Backup and disaster recovery

    Defined recovery time and recovery point targets, tested restores every quarter and runbooks anyone on the team can follow.
